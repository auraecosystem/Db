# Databender privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026

This policy covers the Databender app for Android (package `com.databender.app`), published on Google Play by maelswarm.

> **In short:** Databender does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. Your pictures and videos are bent on your phone and never leave it unless you save or share them yourself. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## Your pictures and videos

- You choose a picture or video with Android's own photo picker or file browser. Databender can only see the files you pick, and it asks for no storage, camera or other permissions.
- All processing happens on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, and the app has no server.
- While you work, the app keeps temporary files (the working copy of a video and the finished result) in its private cache. They are deleted when you open another video or the app starts again, and Android may clear them at any time.
- A picture, video or frame is saved to your gallery (Pictures/Databender or Movies/Databender) only when you tap Save, and handed to another app only when you tap Share and choose that app. What happens to it after that is up to you and that app.

## Purchases

- Databender Pro is a subscription sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; Databender never sees your card or payment details.
- To unlock Pro, the app asks Google Play whether your Google account has an active subscription, and remembers the answer on your phone so Pro works offline. Google's handling of your purchase is covered by the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- Tester codes are checked on the phone. Nothing about them is sent anywhere.

## Settings and backups

The app stores a few settings on your phone, such as whether Pro is unlocked. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include these settings in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. They contain no pictures, videos or personal information.

## No ads, analytics or tracking

Databender contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking code. The only outside component is Google Play Billing, used for the subscription.

## Children

Databender is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, removes everything it kept on your phone. Pictures and videos you saved stay in your gallery until you delete them. To cancel Pro, use Google Play's Payments and subscriptions page.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
